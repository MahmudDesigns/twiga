# Twiga Foods Thika Depot – Official Website

A modern, professional, and mobile-responsive website for **Twiga Foods Thika Depot**, designed to showcase B2B agricultural distribution services, warehouse facilities, and vendor partnership opportunities in Thika, Kenya.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This website was built to establish a strong online presence for Twiga Foods Thika Depot, emphasizing:

- B2B agricultural product distribution
- Well-managed warehouse (godown) facilities
- Easy accessibility from Thika town
- Professional, trustworthy business positioning

The site is optimized for **business inquiries, warehouse rental leads, and vendor partnerships**.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience

- Agricultural product vendors
- Retailers and wholesalers
- Market stall owners
- Businesses seeking reliable godown/warehouse space
- B2B food distribution partners

---

## ✨ Key Features

### Core Pages
- **Homepage** – Value proposition, services overview, testimonials, trust indicators
- **About Us** – Business mission, warehouse overview, location advantages
- **Services** – B2B distribution, warehouse rental, storage & logistics
- **Facilities** – Warehouse specs, parking, security, accessibility
- **Testimonials** – Integrated customer reviews with 4.5⭐ rating
- **Contact** – Inquiry form, Google Maps embed, phone & email details

### Functional Highlights
- Mobile-first responsive design
- SEO-friendly structure (optimized for:
  - *B2B agricultural products*
  - *warehouse Thika*
  - *godown rental Thika*)
- Click-to-call phone numbers on mobile
- Google Maps integration
- Form validation for inquiries
- Clean UI with smooth animations
