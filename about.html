Our Story

Building Bridges in Agricultural Commerce

Twiga Foods Thika Depot was established with a clear vision: to revolutionize the agricultural distribution landscape in Kenya. What started as a modest warehouse facility has grown into a comprehensive B2B hub serving hundreds of vendors and retailers across the region.

Located strategically in Thika, our depot serves as a crucial link between farmers and markets, ensuring that fresh agricultural products reach vendors efficiently and in optimal condition. Our commitment to excellence has made us a trusted name in the industry.

Today, we continue to invest in state-of-the-art facilities, technology, and partnerships that strengthen Kenya's agricultural value chain while supporting the livelihoods of thousands of farmers and vendors.

Our Journey

Key Milestones in Our Growth

2014

Foundation & Launch

Twiga Foods Thika Depot was established with our first warehouse facility, beginning operations to serve local agricultural vendors in Thika.

2017

Expansion & Growth

Expanded our facility to 30,000 sq.ft, added more loading docks, and reached 200+ active vendor partnerships across the region.

2020

Technology Integration

Implemented modern inventory management systems and digital platforms to improve efficiency and vendor experience during challenging times.

2024

Leading the Industry

Now operating 50,000+ sq.ft of warehouse space, serving 500+ vendors, and recognized as a premier B2B agricultural distribution hub in Kenya.
