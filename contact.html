FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about contacting us and visiting our depot.

For urgent inquiries, we recommend calling us directly at +254 700 000 000 or reaching out via WhatsApp for immediate assistance. Our team is available during business hours and typically responds within minutes for urgent matters.
While walk-ins are welcome during business hours, we recommend scheduling an appointment for facility tours, especially if you're interested in warehouse rental. This ensures a dedicated team member can show you available spaces and answer all your questions.
We aim to respond to all email and form inquiries within 24 hours on business days. For faster response, contact us via phone or WhatsApp. Warehouse rental inquiries typically receive priority handling and may get responses even sooner.
Yes! We have ample free parking for all visitors, including space for trucks and large vehicles. Our parking area is well-lit and secure, with 24/7 surveillance. Simply check in with security at the entrance upon arrival.
Yes, we can arrange virtual facility tours via video call for clients who cannot visit in person. Contact us to schedule a virtual tour where our team will show you our warehouse spaces, parking facilities, and answer your questions in real-time.
Our team is fluent in English and Swahili (Kiswahili), ensuring clear communication with all our local and international clients. We're committed to providing excellent service in the language you're most comfortable with.
