Our Mission: Connecting Agricultural Communities

Twiga Foods Thika Depot is a premier B2B agricultural distribution hub strategically located in the heart of Thika. We bridge the gap between agricultural producers and vendors, providing well-managed warehouse facilities that ensure the freshness and quality of products.

Our commitment to excellence has made us the preferred partner for hundreds of vendors and retailers across the region. With spacious facilities, ample parking, and easy accessibility from Thika town, we make agricultural commerce seamless and efficient.
